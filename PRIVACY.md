# Form Saathi — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 31 October 2026*

Form Saathi ("the app") is an independent app made by an individual developer in India. It is **not** a government app and is not affiliated with any government body.

## Summary

**The app does not collect, store or share any of your personal data on any server.** Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

## What the app uses, and why

| What | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Photos you pick or take | To resize and compress them for forms | Processed only on your phone. Never uploaded. |
| Camera (only when you tap "Camera") | To take a photo or signature | Stays on your phone |
| Saving to gallery | To save the finished photo | Your phone's gallery |
| Document names and expiry dates you enter | To show reminders | Stored only on your phone |
| Checklist ticks and language choice | To remember your progress and settings | Stored only on your phone |
| Notifications | To remind you before a document expires | Created on your phone; no server involved |

## What the app does NOT do

- No account or login.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising IDs.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
- The app does not read your messages, contacts or location.

## Links to other websites

Guides contain links to official government websites (for example passportindia.gov.in). When you open a link, that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Sharing

If you tap "Share", your phone's share menu opens and **you** choose where the photo goes (for example WhatsApp). The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

## Deleting your data

Delete a document inside the app, or uninstall the app, or clear the app's storage in your phone's Settings. This removes everything the app stored.

## Children

The app is meant for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
