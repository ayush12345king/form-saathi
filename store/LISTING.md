## Full description — English

Get your government paperwork right the first time.

**📸 Photo & Signature Resizer**
Resize your photo and signature to the exact pixels and KB size a form needs: SSC, IBPS, SBI, UPSC, NEET, US visa, or any custom size. Signature paper is cleaned to white and the ink is made darker. Add your name and date under the photo when a form asks for it. Works offline: your photos never leave your phone.

**📘 29 step-by-step guides in Hindi and English**
• Passport: new, Tatkal, renewal, police verification, child passport, PCC
• Visas: tips that improve approval, Schengen (Europe), USA, UK, Canada, Dubai, visa-free countries, working abroad safely
• ID cards: Aadhaar update, free instant e-PAN, PAN–Aadhaar linking, Voter ID, DigiLocker OTP fixes
• Vehicle: learner's licence test, DL renewal, safe e-challan payment
• Certificates, PF withdrawal, scholarships, exam form mistakes
Every guide has Pro Tips and Common Mistakes that are hard to find elsewhere, plus a link to the official website.

**🔔 Expiry reminders**
Add your DL, RC, PUC, insurance, passport or visa. Get a notification 30, 7 and 1 day before expiry.

**✅ Checklists**
Know exactly what to carry: passport, Tatkal, Schengen visa, US visa interview, learner's licence, exam form, bank account.

**🛡️ Scam alerts**
Learn to spot fake websites, fake challan messages and "digital arrest" calls.

**Private by design:** no login, no tracking, no data collection.

⚠️ Disclaimer: Form Saathi is NOT a government app and is not affiliated with any government entity. Information is collected from official public sources such as passportindia.gov.in, parivahan.gov.in, uidai.gov.in, incometax.gov.in, voters.eci.gov.in and official embassy websites. Always confirm on the official website before you apply.
